Noura Accessories draws its soul from light: the quiet gleam on a small piece that completes a look without raising its voice. Carefully chosen pieces, wrapped like gifts, arriving like a love letter. Noura is a beauty that is soft, warm, and glowing from within.

الشعار الرئيسي

الشعار مكتوب بقلم عريض بخط حر: ذيلا الواو والراء ينسابان كورقتين، ونقطة النون معيّنة تشبه فص الحجر الكريم. يرافقه الاسم اللاتيني NOURA بخط رفيع يوازن ثقل الحروف العربية.

A free-hand broad-nib logotype. The tails of the waw and raa flow like two leaves, the dot of the noon is cut like a gemstone, and the fine Latin NOURA balances the weight of the Arabic letters.
